# Replace @solidjs/meta with an adapter-owned Head component

This adapter previously delegated `<head>` management to `@solidjs/meta` (a peer dependency, with `<App>` wrapping the tree in `MetaProvider`), and an earlier custom `HeadContext.ts` was removed in beta.8 in favor of that package. We now reverse that decision: `@solidjs/meta` is removed entirely and replaced by an adapter-owned `<Head>` component backed by `@inertiajs/core`'s `createHeadManager`, mirroring the official React/Vue adapters.

The driver is Inertia parity, not a Laravel requirement. Inertia owns tag deduplication, provider precedence, server-rendered tag adoption, and DOM patching. `@solidjs/meta` follows Solid's ownership model well, but writes the head itself and deduplicates with its own `data-sm` identities. We borrow its ownership discipline rather than combine two head writers. Keeping intrinsic Inertia JSX also rules out recovering reactive props through Meta's uppercase tag components: Solid's compiled DOM nodes do not expose their original prop getters.

## Consequences

- SolidJS has no client-side JSX-to-string serialization (`renderToString` throws in the browser), so `<Head>` serializes resolved DOM elements to tag strings. Its `children()` accessor is created once on mount, after the body's hydration lookup has finished, and inserted into a detached container. A MutationObserver invalidates an owned effect without reconstructing children or their signal state.
- During SSR, `<Head>` emits an internal template into Solid's render. Extraction selects only templates surviving in the completed HTML, removes hydration attributes, and submits their resolved children to a request-local Inertia head manager. Numeric registration order preserves Inertia's provider precedence independently of body order. Removing templates preserves the remaining body bytes.
- Direct title props are initially read under their Solid owner so enclosing Suspense can track resources. A public `useAssets` callback snapshots the scalar title during renderer finalization, when Solid restores the request's resource context and before root disposal. Disposed branches cancel their snapshots. Retaining getters for use after the renderer's promise resolves would risk reading another concurrent request's resources; replaying children would recreate components and resources.
- Server extraction uses parse5 to handle HTML attributes, raw script/style content, and templates correctly. The parser is loaded only during SSR. This adds parsing work and a dependency; SSR title finalization also relies on Solid's public asset lifecycle.
- Head managers belong to the client App or an individual server response. Provider updates invalidate one client App effect that retracks the global title callback's current signal dependencies, including when no Head provider is mounted.
- Asynchronous rendering can create conflicting Head instances in different orders on the server and client. Preserving Inertia's creation-order precedence does not make those different timelines equivalent.
- This is a breaking change shipped during the beta line; migration notes live in the changelog only.
